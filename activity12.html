⇆ Instagram ⇆

Instagram is a social media platform for sharing photos and videos.


Instagram Facts Part 1

  • Instagram was launched in 2010 by Kevin Systrom and Mike Krieger.
  • It was acquired by Facebook in 2012 for approximately $1 billion.
  • As of 2021, Instagram has over 1 billion monthly active users.
  • Instagram's logo was designed by Cole Rise, a photographer and designer.

Instagram Facts Part 2

  • Instagram's original name was "Burbn" before it was rebranded.
  • The first photo ever posted on Instagram was of a dog and a foot, taken by co-founder Kevin Systrom.
  • Instagram introduced Stories in 2016, allowing users to share temporary content that disappears after 24 hours.
  • In 2020, Instagram launched Reels, a feature for creating and sharing short-form videos.
